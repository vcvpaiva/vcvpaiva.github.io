<p>Mathoscope is the new name of Parmesan, a system for extracting mathematical concepts from text, with a focus on category theory. It was built with Jacob Collard and Eswaran Subrahmanian, using the nLab, the abstracts of Theory and Applications of Categories, and Tom Leinster's Basic Category Theory as its texts.</p>

<h4>Where the idea comes from</h4>

<p>Parmesan grew out of the MathFoldr project on networked mathematics at the Topos Institute, which I described in a series of posts on the Topos blog:</p>
<ul>
  <li><a href="https://topos.institute/blog/2021-07-11-introducing-mathfoldr/">Introducing the MathFoldr Project</a> (with Brendan Fong), July 2021</li>
  <li><a href="https://topos.institute/blog/2022-04-18-facets-of-networked-mathematics/">The many facets of Networked Mathematics</a>, April 2022</li>
  <li><a href="https://topos.institute/blog/2022-11-16-mathematical-concepts/">Mathematical concepts: how do you recognize them?</a> (with Jacob Collard), November 2022</li>
  <li><a href="https://topos.institute/blog/2023-01-05-preparing-for-networked-mathematics/">Preparing for Networked Mathematics</a>, January 2023</li>
</ul>
