title: "Dialectica Constructions"
year: 2026
venue: "Dialectica categories began in my thesis (Cambridge, 1991, https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/techreports/UCAM-CL-TR-213.pdf) as categorical versions of Gödel's Dialectica interpretation. They form a family of models of linear logic: the original Dialectica construction Dial(C) models intuitionistic linear logic as well as the Dialectica interpretation itself, and its Girard variant Gir(C) models classical linear logic. Recent work relates the family to Gödel doctrines and logical principles (with Davide Trotta and Matteo Spadetto), to cardinal invariants in set theory (with Samuel G. da Silva), to Petri nets (with Elena Di Lavore and Wilmer Leal), and to Heyting algebras (with Colin Bloomfield and Peter Jipsen). Lenses, polynomials, games and partial compilers turn out to be dialectica morphisms whose relations have been forgotten, and putting the relations back gives guarantees. Overviews: the CALCO 2021 invited paper Dialectica Comonads (https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol211-calco2021/LIPIcs.CALCO.2021.3/LIPIcs.CALCO.2021.3.pdf) and the Topos blog post Dialectica Categories in Computing (https://topos.institute/blog/2022-12-06-dialectia-in-computing/). Current drafts and notes: https://github.com/vcvpaiva/Manuscripts"
